**Monthly partnership profit** is the periodic return calculated from your chosen package and invested amount. It is the main income stream for most Etemad Melal investors. This guide covers how profit is defined, scheduled, and tracked from commitment through payout.

## Return rate by package

Each tier — Aghaze, Pishgam, Paydar, Abr, and Saramad — has a defined approximate monthly return (roughly 3% to 6%). The exact figure is visible when you purchase the package and in your signed digital contract.

| Package | Approx. monthly return | Term |
|---------|------------------------|------|
| Aghaze | 3% | 18 months |
| Pishgam | 4% | 36 months |
| Paydar | 4.5% | 36 months |
| Abr | 5% | 36 months |
| Saramad | 6% | 36 months |

Returns are contractual frameworks; payment timing and exceptions are specified in official documents and panel records.

## Installment schedule

The **Monthly Profit Schedule** lists installments due on specific dates. Each row shows status:

- **Paid** — credited to your account
- **Unpaid** — upcoming or pending
- **Overdue** — past due per schedule rules

Review this menu at least monthly so you know what was committed versus what was received.

## Financial dashboard view

The dashboard chart **paid monthly profit vs. commitment** shows:

- Total profit paid year-to-date
- Your ongoing monthly obligation based on active packages

A gap in early months can be normal as schedules align; the trend over subsequent months should become clearer. Use this chart for planning withdrawals or reinvestment.

## Distinction from referral and balance

Monthly profit comes from **package capital**. Direct participation and collaborative balance come from **network activity**. All three appear in Commissions with separate type filters — do not assume one total represents “investment return” alone.

## Activation checklist

1. Complete identity verification in the panel.
2. Deposit sufficient funds to your wallet.
3. Choose a package within the allowed amount range.
4. Read and sign the digital contract.
5. Monitor the Monthly Profit Schedule after activation.

If no schedule appears, confirm the package status is **active** in the dashboard. Support tickets can clarify contract-specific questions with your signed document as reference.

Monthly profit is the backbone of the Etemad Melal experience for capital-focused investors. Track it through official panel tools, not informal estimates.

## Reinvest or withdraw?

After profit credits arrive in your wallet, you choose whether to withdraw or purchase another package. Reinvesting extends compound exposure within platform rules; withdrawing supports personal cash-flow needs. Neither choice is universally better — align it with the goals you set before signing.

## When installments differ from expectations

If a due date passes without a paid status, wait for the panel to update, then check contract terms for grace or processing windows. Persistent gaps warrant a support ticket with package name, installment date, and screenshot of the schedule row.
