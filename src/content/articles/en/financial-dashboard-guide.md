The **Financial Dashboard** is the heart of the Etemad Melal user panel. When you understand what each widget represents, managing capital, profit, and network activity becomes much simpler. This guide maps the main sections.

## Wallet balance

The first number you usually see is **available balance**:

- Used to purchase new packages
- Source for withdrawal requests
- Increases after confirmed deposits

Open the transactions menu for full deposit and withdrawal history with status filters.

## Paid profit vs. monthly commitment

A chart or summary card compares:

- Profit **paid to date**
- **Total monthly commitment** from all active packages

In early months, the gap between these figures may be normal as schedules align. Over time, the paid line should track your expectations based on the contract. Use this view for withdrawal planning.

## Active packages

The active packages list includes:

- Package name and invested amount
- Start date
- Status (active, expired, etc.)

From here, navigate to the **Monthly Profit Schedule** for each contract.

## Network participation volume

If you build a network, **left and right branch volume** appears on the dashboard. These metrics feed **balance rewards** — they do not replace package profit. Investors without a team can ignore this section.

## Link to commission detail

The dashboard is a **summary**. Line-by-line reward detail lives in **Commissions**, filtered by:

- Monthly profit
- Referral
- Balance

| Dashboard shows | Commissions shows |
|-----------------|-------------------|
| Totals and trends | Individual rows, sources, status |
| Branch volume | Per-event balance credits |
| Active package list | Linked profit entries |

## Recommended weekly routine

1. Check balance and any pending transactions.
2. Review profit due dates for the coming week in the schedule.
3. If you lead a network, skim referral and balance activity for the week.

This simple routine prevents surprises and keeps your mental model aligned with panel data.

## Getting started

Log in at [panel.etemadmelal.com](https://panel.etemadmelal.com) and open the financial dashboard from the menu. If sections appear empty, you may not yet have an active package — begin with **Choose Package** after verification and wallet funding.

The dashboard is your daily command center. Pair it with commission filters whenever you need to drill into a specific reward or dispute an entry.

## Mobile and multi-package users

If you hold more than one active package, the commitment chart aggregates obligations across contracts. Check each package row individually when planning withdrawals so you do not overestimate available profit for a single tier.

Network builders should glance at branch volume whenever reviewing referral spikes — a surge on one side may explain why balance rewards lag even when direct referral income looks strong.
