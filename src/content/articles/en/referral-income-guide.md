**Referral** (direct participation reward) is income credited to your account based on activity from people who joined through your introduction path. It is separate from monthly package profit and from balance rewards. This guide explains how referral works in the Etemad Melal panel.

## What is a referral code?

After you create your **position** in the panel, you receive a referral code. When new users sign up, entering your code links them to your network. Important: referral and upline codes usually **cannot be changed** after registration — share your code carefully and verify spelling before others submit it.

## How rewards are recorded

In **Commissions**, apply the filter for type **Referral** to see only direct participation rewards. Each row includes:

- Amount and date
- Source (linked member or activity)
- Status: active or ignored

You do not need to track rewards manually. The panel logs every entry so you can compare months or investigate discrepancies via support tickets.

## Referral plus balance

Referral focuses on **direct introductions**. **Balance** focuses on **even growth** across left and right branch volume. Active network participants often earn from both:

| Stream | Primary driver |
|--------|----------------|
| Referral | Direct downline activity |
| Balance | Balanced branch volume |

If you are not building a team, referral income may remain zero. That does not affect your package profit schedule.

## Getting started responsibly

Before inviting others:

1. Complete your own panel setup and identity verification.
2. Understand package terms so you can answer basic questions accurately.
3. Share your referral code only with people who make their own informed decision.

Referral rewards depend on platform rules and downstream activity — they are not guaranteed fixed amounts. Read official materials rather than informal promises from third parties.

## Weekly review habit

- Open Commissions → filter Referral → note new active rows.
- Cross-check totals with the financial dashboard summary.
- If a expected reward is missing, open a ticket with date and member reference.

Referral income can complement package returns for those who choose to grow a network. For everyone else, monthly partnership profit remains the core stream — and the panel keeps each type clearly separated in reports.

## Ethics of sharing your code

Only share referral codes with people who can evaluate the platform independently. Misrepresenting returns or pressure tactics harm trust and may conflict with platform policies. Your long-term referral income depends on informed partners who stay active — not on one-time signups.

## Troubleshooting missing referral credit

Confirm the new member entered your code at registration. If they did and no commission appears after qualifying activity, collect their username and signup date for a support ticket. The Commissions log remains the authoritative record.
