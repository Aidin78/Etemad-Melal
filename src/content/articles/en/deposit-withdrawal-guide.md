Cash flow management on Etemad Melal happens through your **wallet** and the **deposit / withdrawal** section in the user panel. Transparency in this area is the foundation of trust in the platform. This guide walks through each step so you know what to expect.

## Depositing to your wallet

1. From the financial menu, select **Deposit**.
2. Enter the amount and payment method according to the panel instructions.
3. After confirmation, your balance updates in the **Financial Dashboard**.

Until final approval, the transaction appears as **pending**. Do not assume funds are available for package purchase until the status changes to confirmed.

## Requesting a withdrawal

To withdraw profit or available balance:

- Submit the request from the same financial section.
- Destination account details must match your verified identity information.
- After review, status changes to **paid** or **rejected**.

If a request is rejected, the reason is shown in the panel or can be followed up via a support ticket. Keep your banking details current to avoid delays.

## Tracking transaction history

In the transaction list you can filter by:

- Type: deposit / withdrawal
- Date range
- Status (pending, approved, rejected, paid)

This report helps you compare monthly cash flow with **monthly partnership profit** and **network rewards** recorded in commission reports.

## Typical workflow

| Step | Action | Where to check |
|------|--------|----------------|
| 1 | Deposit funds | Wallet balance |
| 2 | Purchase package | Active packages list |
| 3 | Receive monthly profit | Monthly Profit Schedule |
| 4 | Request withdrawal | Transaction history |

## Important notes

Before buying a package, ensure you have sufficient wallet balance. Package purchase and contract activation happen in **Choose Package**, not directly from the deposit form.

Withdrawal processing times depend on review and banking procedures. Check status in the panel rather than assuming instant transfer.

For questions about a specific transaction, use a **panel ticket** or the contact form on the website. Include the transaction ID and date for faster support.

## Security and best practices

- Verify you are on the official panel URL before entering payment details.
- Do not share wallet credentials or one-time codes with anyone claiming to be support outside official channels.
- Match withdrawal destination accounts to the identity you verified at signup — mismatches are a common cause of rejection.

## Planning cash flow around profit

Many investors schedule withdrawals shortly after confirmed profit credits appear in the schedule. Others reinvest wallet balance into additional packages. Either approach works if it aligns with your contract and personal budget — the panel simply provides the transparent ledger to support your choice.
