The Etemad Melal user panel is your hub for investment management, rewards, and financial reporting. If you are new to the platform, follow this recommended path from registration to your first package and beyond.

## Step 1: Sign up and verify identity

Create your account at [panel.etemadmelal.com](https://panel.etemadmelal.com) and complete identity verification. Without verification, package purchase and withdrawals are not available. Keep your contact and banking details accurate — they are used for contract records and payout processing.

## Step 2: Choose an investment package

Five package tiers — from **Aghaze** to **Saramad** — offer different amount ranges, monthly returns, and contract terms. Before buying:

- Compare tiers on the website Packages section
- Confirm your amount fits the allowed range
- Read the digital contract before signing

Aghaze (18 months, from 10M Toman) suits most first-time investors. Longer 36-month tiers target larger capital and extended horizons.

## Step 3: Fund your wallet

Deposit through the financial menu. Wait for confirmation before purchasing a package. Package activation uses wallet balance via **Choose Package**, not the deposit form alone.

## Step 4: Set referral and upline (optional)

At registration you may enter a **referral code** and **upline**. These fields typically **cannot be changed** later — double-check before submitting. If you are not joining through a sponsor, follow panel guidance for default placement.

## Step 5: Track your income streams

The financial dashboard and Commissions section show three reward types separately:

| Stream | Typical first-time experience |
|--------|------------------------------|
| Monthly partnership profit | Active after package purchase |
| Direct participation (referral) | Zero until you refer others |
| Collaborative balance | Zero until network volume qualifies |

## Step 6: Build a review habit

- **Weekly:** wallet balance, pending transactions
- **Monthly:** Monthly Profit Schedule vs. dashboard commitment chart
- **Quarterly:** contract term progress and goal review

## Support and learning

Use panel tickets for account-specific issues. Browse the Articles section on the website for guides on deposits, contracts, transparency, and each income stream.

Your path into Etemad Melal is intentionally straightforward: verify, fund, choose a package, sign the contract, then monitor everything through the panel. Start there, read before you sign, and let official reports — not informal channels — guide your decisions.

## What the panel is not

The panel is not a substitute for personal financial advice. It is an operational tool: contracts, wallets, schedules, and commission logs. Use website articles and FAQ for education; use tickets for account-specific help.

## Next steps after your first package

Once active, read the guides on monthly profit tracking, deposit and withdrawal flow, and — if relevant — the three income streams overview. Each article maps directly to a menu inside the panel so you spend less time guessing and more time reviewing verified data.
