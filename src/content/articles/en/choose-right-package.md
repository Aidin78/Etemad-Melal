Choosing a package is your first major financial decision on Etemad Melal. Five tiers are available, each with different capital ranges, monthly returns, and contract terms. This comparison helps you match a package to your goals and capacity.

## Aghaze — cautious start

- **Range:** 10 – 100M Toman
- **Monthly return:** ~3%
- **Term:** 18 months
- **Best for:** First entry and learning the panel

Aghaze offers the shortest contract term, making it suitable if you want to evaluate the platform before a longer commitment.

## Pishgam and Paydar — mid-tier growth

| Package | Range | Monthly return | Term |
|---------|-------|----------------|------|
| Pishgam | 100 – 500M Toman | ~4% | 36 months |
| Paydar | 500M – 1B Toman | ~4.5% | 36 months |

These tiers suit investors who are comfortable with a three-year horizon and have capital in the mid-range.

## Abr and Saramad — higher tiers

- **Abr:** 1 – 5B Toman, ~5% monthly, 36 months
- **Saramad:** 5 – 100B Toman, ~6% monthly, 36 months

Daily cap and cap multiplier for each package are shown on the package selection page inside the panel. Review these limits before committing large amounts.

## Decision checklist

1. Invest only an amount within the **allowed range** of your chosen package.
2. Consider **contract term** — Aghaze is 18 months; others are 36 months.
3. Monthly return is **approximate**; final terms appear in the official contract.
4. **Referral** and **balance** income are separate from package profit and depend on network activity.

## Matching package to profile

| Investor profile | Suggested starting point |
|------------------|-------------------------|
| New to Etemad Melal | Aghaze |
| Experienced, mid capital | Pishgam or Paydar |
| Large capital, long horizon | Abr or Saramad |

After selection, purchase and contract signing happen in **Choose Package** within the panel. Read the contract before signing, then track your active package in the financial dashboard.

## Upgrading or changing tiers later

Life circumstances and capital levels change. If you outgrow Aghaze or want a higher return tier, review whether a new package purchase fits your wallet balance and contract obligations. Existing contracts remain governed by their signed terms until they expire — do not assume automatic upgrades.

Consult support before making large moves so you understand how daily caps, cap multipliers, and overlapping contracts interact in the panel.

## Questions to ask yourself

- Can this capital remain committed for the full **18 or 36 months**?
- Does the approximate monthly return match my planning assumptions (understanding it is contractual, not guaranteed)?
- Am I choosing this tier for package profit alone, or do I also plan to build referral and balance income?

Honest answers reduce regret later. The right package is the one that fits both your wallet and your timeline — not necessarily the highest percentage on the chart.
