Investing on any platform — including Etemad Melal — should be based on **personal planning**, not momentary excitement. This guide offers a simple framework for making better decisions before you choose a package or commit capital.

## Invest only spare capital

Put in an amount that, if locked until the end of the **contract term** (18 or 36 months depending on the package), will not disrupt your daily life, emergency fund, or essential expenses.

The **Aghaze** package is designed for entry with 10–100 million Toman. Higher tiers — Pishgam, Paydar, Abr, and Saramad — are intended for investors with more experience and financial capacity. Starting smaller is not a weakness; it is responsible risk management.

## Monthly return is not a guaranteed outcome

Each package shows an approximate monthly return rate (3% to 6%). These figures represent the **contractual framework**, not a promise of fixed results in every period. Payment conditions, due dates, and exceptions are clarified in the official contract and the panel.

Use the **Monthly Profit Schedule** to see which installments are due and which have been paid. Compare that data with the financial dashboard chart of paid profit versus monthly commitment.

## Separate package profit from network income

| Type | Source |
|------|--------|
| Monthly partnership profit | Active package capital |
| Direct participation reward | Referrals and team activity |
| Collaborative balance | Balanced network growth |

Package profit depends on your investment amount and chosen tier. Referral and balance rewards depend on network activity — they may be zero, and that is normal for investors who focus only on packages.

## Know the contract before you sign

Before purchasing, read the digital contract in the panel. Pay attention to:

- Contract term and early withdrawal rules
- Daily cap and cap multiplier for your package
- How profit installments are scheduled

If any clause is unclear, open a support ticket **before** signing. Informed consent leads to a calmer investment experience.

## Avoid common mistakes

- **Investing borrowed money** or funds needed for rent, debt, or medical expenses
- **Expecting network income** without building or participating in a team
- **Ignoring panel reports** and relying on informal promises from third parties

Etemad Melal provides transparent reporting — deposits, withdrawals, profit, and commissions — so you can verify status yourself. Responsible investing starts with honest self-assessment, realistic expectations, and regular review of official panel data.
